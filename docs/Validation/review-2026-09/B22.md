- Fix: additive `CallSomaticMutationsMutect2` (+ `CalculateMutect2TumorLog10Odds`, `CalculateMutect2NormalLog10Odds`, `Mutect2SomaticCall`); emit TLOD>3.0, Somatic iff NLOD>2.2 with matched normal. Also XML-doc fix of private score formula (max(0, f_t − f_n)).
