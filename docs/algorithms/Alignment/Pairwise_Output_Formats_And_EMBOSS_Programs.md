# Pairwise alignment output formats and the EMBOSS programs needle, water and stretcher

**Units:** ALIGN-GLOBAL-001, ALIGN-LOCAL-001, ALIGN-MULTI-001 (audit A2-27 / A2-28 / A2-29, B09 F120 - F126).
**Code:** `EmbossAlignmentWriter`, `EmbossAlignFormat`, `EmbossAlignOptions`, `EmbossPairAlignment`, `EmbossAlignedSequence` (`EmbossAlignmentTypes.cs`), `SequenceAligner.StretcherAlign`, `SequenceAligner.NeedleAlign`, `SequenceAligner.WaterAlign`.
**MCP:** `write_alignment`, `needle_align`, `water_align`, `stretcher_align`, `supermatcher_align`, `needleall_align`, `ksw2_extend`, and the `format` / `names` parameters of the pairwise and multiple-alignment tools.

## 1. EMBOSS `-aformat` writers (F120)

`EmbossAlignmentWriter` reproduces the 29 alignment formats of EMBOSS 6.6.0 (`ajax/core/ajalign.c` `alignFormat[]`) byte for byte:

* pairwise layouts (exactly two rows): `pair`, `simple`, `unknown`, `multiple`, `srspair`, `srs`, `markx0`, `markx1`, `markx2`, `markx3`, `markx10`, `match`, `score`, `sam`, `tcoffee`. They print the program header (`Program`, `Rundate`, `Commandline`, `Align_format`, `Report_file`), the sequence list (with `-aaccshow` / `-adesshow` / `-ausashow`), `Matrix`, `Gap_penalty`, `Extend_penalty`, the statistics block (`Length`, `Identity`, `Similarity`, `Gaps`, `Score`, computed in single precision as `ajAlignSetSubStandard` does) and, once per file, the tail;
* sequence-set layouts (any number of rows, the `ajseqwrite.c` writers): `fasta`, `a2m`, `msf` (GCG checksums, `CompCheck`), `clustal` (CLUSTAL W 1.83 layout), `mega`, `meganon`, `nexus` / `paup`, `nexusnon` / `paupnon`, `phylip`, `phylipnon`, `selex`, `treecon`.

Facts established from the source and confirmed by the binaries: the number columns of `markx0` use the `%*d` running widths of `alignWriteMark` (the `num[]` flag persists over blocks); `markx2` shows the second row with `.` where it equals the first; the T-Coffee percent identity is 0 unless the program stored statistics (stretcher does); stretcher prints integer scores and drops the descriptions; EMBOSS prints dates as `Thu 19 Nov 2026 21:43:53` (`%2d` day) in headers and `dd/mm/yy` in MSF / MEGA / NEXUS; the type that MSF / NEXUS / MEGA print comes from the output set (`seqret`) or from the residues (alignment programs: NEXUS always `datatype=DNA`, MEGA `Protein` for every protein-compatible text); `ajStrAssignSubS` clamps a start behind the end to the last character (MEGA blocks of rows of different length).

Verification: `emboss_aformat_oracle.tsv` (`scripts/alignment/generate_emboss_aformat_oracle.py`): needle / water / stretcher, 29 formats x 114 alignment sets (all matrices, gap penalties, `-awidth`, `-aglobal`, `-adesshow`, `-aaccshow`, `-ausashow`, one sequence against up to three, faked run dates through `scripts/alignment/faketime.c`), 3 303 files identical; `emboss_seqout_oracle.tsv` (`generate_emboss_seqout_oracle.py`): `seqret -osformat` of 11 formats on gapped sets of 1 - 9 rows, 1 309 files identical. Not implemented (EMBOSS needs the `cons` consensus): the pair-markup formats for three or more sequences; `debug` (memory trace); `mase` is not an `-aformat`.

## 2. stretcher (F121)

`StretcherAlign` ports `emboss/stretcher.c`: the Myers-Miller (1988) linear-space divide-and-conquer with the program's integer arithmetic, its midpoint rules (`CC + RR >= midc` with the gap-closed test, then the type-2 scan from the right), the edit-script macros and `stretcher_Calcons`. A gap of length k costs `gapopen + (k - 1) * gapextend`; defaults 16 / 4 (nucleotide, EDNAFULL) and 12 / 2 (protein, EBLOSUM62). Oracle `stretcher_oracle.tsv`: 689 / 689 pairs equal (rows and score).

## 3. needle and water (F122)

`NeedleAlign` ports `embAlignPathCalcWithEndGapPenalties` + `embAlignWalkNWMatrixUsingCompass` in single precision (float matrices, gap penalties rounded by `ajRoundFloat` to 16 mantissa bits, so 0.1 becomes 0.10000038), including `-endweight`, `-endopen`, `-endextend` and the traceback tie rules (`U_FEPS` 1.192e-6). `WaterAlign` ports `embAlignPathCalcSW` + `embAlignWalkSWMatrix` (float path, double maxima, the last cell within `U_FEPS` of the maximum, gap walk-back within 0.01). Oracles `needle_oracle.tsv` (887 / 887 pairs, 100+ with end weights) and `water_oracle.tsv` (860 / 860 pairs, rows, starts and scores).

Both programs are not always optimal: their recurrences forbid some gap-state transitions and mis-initialise the boundary of a one-residue sequence (needle prints score 0), so the plain affine optima (`GlobalAlignAffine`, `SemiGlobalAlignAffine(EndGaps.All)`, `LocalAlignAffine`) are upper bounds that the programs reach on more than 90 % of the committed pairs.

## 4. ksw2-style dual affine gaps and band (F123)

`GlobalAlignDualAffine` / `LocalAlignDualAffine` (`DualAffineScoring`, `SequenceAligner.DualAffine.cs`) score a gap of length k as `max(open1 + k ext1, open2 + k ext2)` (the two-piece model of ksw2 `ksw2_extd2`, minimap2 default `-O 4,24 -E 2,1` = `DualAffineScoring.Minimap2`) with Gotoh's O(m n) recurrence over H, E1, F1, E2, F2 and an optional diagonal band (ksw2 `w`: cells |i - j| <= band). Oracle `dual_affine_oracle.tsv`: 1 600 cases (global / local x full / banded, 400 each), scores equal Biopython 1.88 `PairwiseAligner` with the callable gap score and an independent Waterman-Smith-Beyer program (banded variant), every traceback re-scores to its score and its coordinates are consistent. The original ksw2 sources were not compiled or compared; z-drop, end bonus and extension alignment are registered as A2-29b.

## 5. supermatcher and needleall (F124)

`SequenceAligner.Supermatcher` ports `supermatcher.c` with `embword.c` (`embWordGetTable` incl. its window-skipping of `N` / `X`, `embWordRabinKarpInit`, `embWordRabinKarpSearch` with the `lastlocation` cursor and the match extension, `embWordMatchFirstMax`) and `embaln.c` (`embAlignPathCalcSWFast`, `embAlignWalkSWMatrixFast`, `embAlignReportLocal`): the longest word match of a query against the targets fixes a diagonal, a Smith-Waterman of `-width` cells runs along it from the sequence starts to the first sequence end (single precision, `ajRoundFloat` gap penalties), reported when its score is above `-minscore`; the error file text (`No wordmatch start points for A vs B. No alignment`) is returned. The program is a heuristic, not an optimum. The formats that do not show sequences (`match`, `score`) get the plain-rows alignment with the TARGET first (`ajAlignDefineCC`). `SequenceAligner.NeedleAll` = every B sequence against every A sequence by `NeedleAlign` (minimum score default 0: the ACD help says 1.0, the value is 0; error line `Alignment score (S) is less than minimum score(M) for sequences A vs B`); `-nobrief` is `SequenceAligner.SimilaritySubHeader` (`embAlignCalcSimilarity`; a pair without gap-free column prints the x86 `-nan`). The score table of needle / water / supermatcher is one class (`EmbossScoreTable`). Oracle `emboss_multi_oracle.tsv` (`generate_emboss_multi_oracle.py`): 260 supermatcher + 260 needleall runs, whole output files in 12 `-aformat`s and the error files, byte for byte, 520 / 520.

## 6. ksw2 extension alignment (F125)

`Ksw2Aligner.ExtendZ` / `ExtendD` (scalar `ksw_extz` / `ksw_extd`) and `ExtendZ2` / `ExtendD2` (`ksw_extz2_sse` / `ksw_extd2_sse`, minimap2 v2.26) give the global or extension alignment with `q + k e` or `min(q + k e, q2 + k e2)` gap cost, band `w`, Z-drop (`ksw_apply_zdrop`: row-wise for the scalar functions, per anti-diagonal for the SIMD ones), end bonus, the ambiguous-base score (`mat[m*m-1]`, `-e` / `-e2` when 0) and the CIGAR (`ksw_backtrack`, left / right gap placement, reversed output). The SIMD functions are ported lane by lane (16-lane blocks past the band, byte wrap-around, one `mem` buffer with the C layout). Verified against an independent Gotoh program (max, mqe, mte, score, every CIGAR re-scored along its path; 330 cases per variant). Facts: the SIMD band is blurred to 16-lane blocks and `w = 0` ends at the second anti-diagonal; `e2 == e` leaves the long-gap boundary values undefined; the real C program is needed for Z-drop, the approximate flags and banded SIMD cases: `docs/Validation/review-2026-09/handoff/A2-29b-ksw2` (Docker package).

## 7. Biopython `Bio.Align` text formats (F126)

`AlignmentFormats` (+ `BioAlignment`, `BioAlignmentRecord`, `AlignmentWriteOptions`, `AlignmentFileFormat`) writes `fasta`, `a2m`, `clustal`, `phylip`, `nexus`, `stockholm` byte for byte as Biopython 1.88 `Bio.Align.write` and reads them as `Bio.Align.parse` (ids, descriptions, printed rows, `alignment.annotations`, `column_annotations`, record annotations, letter annotations, dbxrefs; all-gap columns disappear as in `Alignment.parse_printed_alignment`). Python `textwrap.fill` (Stockholm long text) is ported (`PythonTextWrap`). Oracle `biopython_msa_oracle.tsv`: 210 written alignment sets per format (1 - 3 alignments, 1 - 8 rows, up to 1 500 columns, quoted ids, descriptions, Stockholm GF / GS / GR / GC annotation, nexus interleave) and the files Biopython can read back (a2m 210, nexus 210, stockholm 197, fasta 128, phylip 125, clustal 124). The EMBOSS writers (`EmbossAlignmentWriter`) and the ClustalW program layouts (`ClustalWAlignmentWriter`) are different dialects (widths, headers, name rules) and are not merged: the MCP `format` parameter selects between them (`emboss:` prefix for the formats the two share: fasta, a2m, clustal, nexus, phylip).

## 8. Reading the input sequences like EMBOSS (A4-3, B09 F153)

Every EMBOSS port reads a PLAIN input sequence through one shared cleaner, `EmbossSequenceReader.Clean` (Infrastructure): only the letters `A-Z a-z` and `*` survive; `-` `.` `~` `?` `#` `+` `_`, digits, blanks, punctuation, control characters and every non-ASCII character are dropped BEFORE any length check, coordinate or score, so positions and `Length` count the cleaned sequence (`needle` of `AC-GTTAGC.TT*` and `ACGTTAGCTT`: score 58, `Length: 11`; the gapped text scored 43 before). Source (EMBOSS 6.6, `ajax/core`): `ajseqread.c` `seqAppend` keeps letters and `* . ~ ? # + -`, `ajseqtype.c` `ajSeqTypeCheckIn` calls `ajStrRemoveGap` for every ungapped ACD type (`any`, `dna`, `nucleotide`, `protein`, `stopprotein`: all the programs below) and `ajstr.c` `ajStrRemoveGap` keeps exactly `A-Z a-z *`. Measured on the installed binaries (`scripts/alignment/measure_emboss_reader_chars.py`): with any of the 252 other characters inserted into a sequence the output of needle, water, stretcher, matcher, merger, megamerger, seqmatchall, fuzznuc, fuzzpro, fuzztran, etandem, equicktandem, dotmatcher and palindrome is identical to the output without it (199 characters dropped, both sequences, nucleotide and protein). A sequence that is empty afterwards is "Unable to read sequence" (zero length) in every program.

The programs that read an ALIGNMENT (consambig, showalign, cons, distmat; ACD type `gapany`) keep `. ~ -` as gaps and read `? # +` as the unknown residue (the three are interchangeable: measured), every other non-letter is dropped (`EmbossSequenceReader.CleanGapped`); rows that become unequal are padded with trailing gaps as the program does.

Ports using the cleaner: `NeedleAlign`, `WaterAlign`, `StretcherAlign`, `Merger`, `NeedleAll`, `Supermatcher`, `LocalAlign(Affine)TopN(.., TopNTieOrder.Sim)` (matcher), `DotMatcher.Compute`, `GenomicAnalyzer.MegaMerge`, `SeqMatchAll`, `FindPatternHits` (string overloads; fuzznuc), `FindProteinPatternHits` (fuzzpro), `FindTranslatedPatternHits` (fuzztran), `FindTandemRepeatsEtandem` / `FindTandemRepeatsEquicktandem`, `MotifFinder.GenerateEmbossConsensus` / `GenerateEmbossAmbiguousConsensus`, `MultipleAlignmentDisplay.ShowAlign`, `MultipleAlignmentStatistics.CalculateDistanceMatrix` (distmat). The non-EMBOSS-named entry points (`GlobalAlign`, `LocalAlign`, `FindMotif`, the `DnaSequence` overloads ...) keep every character literally. `EmbossSequenceReader.IsNucleotide` is the single `ajSeqIsNuc` rule (default matrix / alphabet of the programs above; it replaces the copies in `DotMatcher`, `MotifFinder`, `StretcherAlign`).

Oracle `emboss_gap_oracle.tsv` (`generate_emboss_gap_oracle.py`): 18 programs x 240 rows whose inputs contain such characters (4 320 rows) equal on all (`EmbossGapCharacters_Oracle_Tests`).

## 9. Sequence-input qualifiers (A5-7, B09 F172)

Every EMBOSS program reads its sequences through the ACD sequence types, which accept `-sbegin -send -sreverse -slower -supper -scircular -snucleotide -sprotein` (and `-sformat`, `-sask`) for each input (`-sbegin1`, `-sbegin2`, ... for two inputs). The ports implement them ONCE: `EmbossSequenceQualifiers` (Infrastructure record; `Parse("-sbegin1 3 -send1 -2 -sreverse1")` reads a command-line fragment) and `EmbossInputSequence.Read` (Alignment: `ajSeqTypeCheckIn`, `ajSeqFmtUpper/Lower`, `ajSeqReverseDo`, `ajSeqTrim`, `ajCvtSposToPosStart`), returning an `EmbossSequenceView` (trimmed residues, `Offset`, `Offend`, `Reversed`, ...). Sources: EMBOSS 6.6 `ajacd.c`, `ajseq.c`, `ajseqread.c`, `ajseqtype.c`, `ajmath.c`, `ajalign.c`, the 16 program sources (raw.githubusercontent.com/kimrutherford/EMBOSS), all measured on the installed 6.6.0.

* **Range**: 1-based, negative = from the end, clipped into the sequence, an end before the begin gives one residue; `-sreverse` reverse-complements a NUCLEOTIDE sequence (case kept, `X` -> `N`, `U` and `T` -> `A`; a protein is never reversed) and mirrors the range, so the range refers to the original strand.
* **Forced type**: `-snucleotide` / `-sprotein` set the sequence type; under a nucleotide ACD type (`dna`, `nucleotide`) a protein letter of a sequence forced to nucleotide becomes `N`, `-sprotein` is refused ("Sequence is not nucleic"), a protein ACD type refuses `-snucleotide`; the second sequence of an alignment is `nucleotide` or `stopprotein` by the type of the first.
* **Reported coordinates**: the alignment report counts the ORIGINAL strand (a reversed sequence is numbered down: `b 36 ... 1`; the two offsets swap sides, `EmbossAlignedSequence.WithView`). The later sequences of a `seqall` stream carry the first sequence's offsets when only an end is given (the AJAX object is reused: measured); a `seqset` decides the reversal once from its first member.
* **Per program** (all measured): needle / water / stretcher / matcher / supermatcher / needleall (`NeedleProgram`, `WaterProgram`, `StretcherProgram`, `MatcherProgram`, `Supermatcher` / `NeedleAll` with `EmbossSequence.Qualifiers`): the alignment of the trimmed sequences, offsets added, `MatrixName` / default penalties by the first sequence's type; fuzznuc / fuzzpro (`FindPatternHits(.., qualifiers, ..)`, `FindProteinPatternHits`): search the range, report the ORIGINAL strand (the strand flips for `-sreverse`); fuzztran: cuts the reversed text at the begin / end AS GIVEN (not mirrored), numbers from its first position, prints negative numbers for the reverse frames (`S - |frame| - 3 start + 4`; frame -3 of `-frame R / 6` uses the END as `S`: a defect of the program, reproduced); etandem ignores `-sbegin` / `-send` and numbers a reversed sequence on the reverse strand; equicktandem searches the range and adds the begin; dotmatcher scans the WHOLE length over the cut text (the range, a NUL, then the old text behind it); seqmatchall ignores the range and only LABELS a reversed set (words matched on the original, positions numbered from the end); distmat ignores the range and reverse-complements every row of a nucleotide set.
* **merger / megamerger** (`SequenceAligner.MergerProgram`, `GenomicAnalyzer.MegaMerge(EmbossSequence, EmbossSequence, ..)`; measured): merger does NOT trim - the whole (possibly reverse-complemented, case-changed) sequences are merged, `-sbegin` / `-send` have no effect, the conflict positions count the sequences as used, a protein pair uses BLOSUM62; megamerger (ACD type `dna`) merges the TRIMMED texts (range cut after the reversal) and its report counts positions of the trimmed texts; both write the merged sequence in lower case with the conflict bases upper case.
* **Not covered**: `-scircular` has no effect on any of the sixteen programs (measured); `-sformat` (the formats of the residue strings are B20's parsers).

Oracle `emboss_seqopts_oracle.tsv` (`generate_emboss_seqopts_oracle.py`): 16 programs x 180 rows with random begin / end (inside, at, beyond the ends, negative, zero, reversed ranges), reverse, case, circular and type overrides, also the refused combinations: all equal (`EmbossSequenceQualifiers_Oracle_Tests`). MCP parameter `sequenceQualifiers` of `needle_align`, `water_align`, `stretcher_align`, `supermatcher_align`, `needleall_align`, `dotmatcher`, `find_pattern_hits`, `fuzzpro`, `fuzztran`, `find_tandem_repeats_emboss`, `seqmatchall`, `alignment_distance_matrix`.

### 9.1 The qualifier TEXT of the MCP tools (A6-2, B09 F177) and matcher / supermatcher options (A6-3, A6-5)

The text of `sequenceQualifiers` is what follows the sequence arguments of the command line, resolved by the ACD library of the program (`AcdCommandLine`, a port of `acdArgsParse` / `acdIsQual` / `acdFindQual*` over the qualifier universe of each program, `EmbossAcdProfiles.g.cs`; `EmbossSequenceQualifiers.ParseInputs(program, text)` returns one entry per sequence input). Rules, all measured: names are case-insensitive, `-` / `--` / `/` prefixes, `-sbegin=5` (also `sbegin=5`), `-nosreverse1`, boolean values `y yes t true 1` / `n no f false 0` (a separate value token only when it is one of those words), integers `strtol` decimal saturated to int; a number selects the input (`-sbegin1`; `1` is valid for a one-input program); an UNNUMBERED qualifier belongs to the sequence parameter that precedes it (after two files: the second, `-sbegin 3` = `-sbegin2 3`), a numbered qualifier of another input or a qualifier of the program ends that tie, afterwards (and for an abbreviation that the general search resolves to another item, `-sbe 3`) an unnumbered qualifier applies to ALL inputs; an abbreviation must be unique among ALL qualifiers of the program (`-se` is ambiguous in a one-sequence program, `-s` always); `-sformat -sask -squick -sdbname -sid -iquery -ufo -fformat -fopenfile` are accepted without effect, `-ioffset` must be 0, any other qualifier or a stray word is refused. Oracles: `emboss_acd_args_oracle.tsv` (2 080 rows from the program's ACD processing log), `emboss_seqopts_text_oracle.tsv` (640 report rows).

`MatcherProgram` takes `-gapopen`, `-gapextend`, `-alternatives` as the ACD integers (below the minimum: the program's warning in `EmbossProgramResult.StandardError` and the minimum; non-integer: `ArgumentException`); `-gapopen` below `-gapextend` is legal (SIM's `q = gapopen - gapextend` < 0; `matcher_gap_oracle.tsv`, 872 rows). `Supermatcher` with a word length above every target returns the program's `Error: no kmers found` (`StandardError`) without the Rabin-Karp loops (`supermatcher_wordlen_oracle.tsv`, 260 rows).


## 10. Feature-report texts (A5-8, B09 F176)

`EmbossReportWriter` (Infrastructure) writes `-rformat gff` and `-rformat excel` for fuzznuc, fuzzpro, fuzztran, etandem and equicktandem from the `EmbossReport` that `EmbossReports.Fuzznuc / Fuzzpro / Fuzztran / Etandem / Equicktandem` build from the hits: one canonical layer, no per-program writer. Facts measured on 6.6.0: fuzznuc / fuzzpro / fuzztran write NOTHING when there is no hit, etandem / equicktandem write the header; the gff `##sequence-region` end is the sequence length (fuzznuc, etandem, equicktandem) or the largest coordinate read as an unsigned 32-bit integer (fuzzpro, fuzztran: their feature table has no length; negative reverse-frame coordinates give numbers near 2^32); attribute values are percent-encoded (`,` = `%2C`); a zero score is `.` in gff; a pattern with a range element `X(n,m)` lists its hits start-descending (fuzznuc without mismatches, fuzzpro, fuzztran) and the complement strand end-descending; fuzznuc's score is the hit length minus the mismatches. Oracle `emboss_report_oracle.tsv`: 120 runs per program and format (1 200 reports, whole text compared, `#!Date` masked): all equal (`EmbossReport_Oracle_Tests`). MCP `reportFormat` / `sequenceName` of `find_pattern_hits`, `fuzzpro`, `fuzztran`, `find_tandem_repeats_emboss`.
